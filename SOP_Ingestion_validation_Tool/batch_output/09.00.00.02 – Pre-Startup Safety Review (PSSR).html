Establish the requirements for performing and documenting Pre-Startup Safety Reviews (PSSRs). A PSSR will confirm, at a minimum, that: Construction and modifications are performed in accordance with design specifications and meet the requirements specified by the associated Management of Change (MOC), if applicable. Recommendations from a Process Hazard Analysis (PHA) or Hazard Screening Review (HSR) are resolved or implemented. Equipment and assets are inspected, tested, calibrated, etc., in accordance with design specifications. Affected employees are notified or trained. The asset is ready for commissioning and startup. Post startup deliverables are identified and assigned. A PSSR is required: In conjunction with every MOC. Prior to startup of any new asset. Prior to startup of an existing asset has been out of service for any length of time. Prior to startup of an existing asset has been in standby for 12 or more months. Before returning existing assets to service when removed from service for: Non-routine repairs Significant maintenance Otherwise disassembled (e.g., cavern workover, compressor overhaul, equipment, piping, vessels etc. which were opened or disassembled for inspection etc.) A PSSR may be required even if an MOC is not. For additional guidance on effectively executing the PSSR process for large-scale projects or for phased startup of complex projects or jobs, see MOC Guidance. Applies to: All Company Facilities
