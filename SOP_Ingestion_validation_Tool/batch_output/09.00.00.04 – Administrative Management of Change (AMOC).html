MOC Originator: INITIATE the Management of Change (MOC) in Maximo, completing all required fields. In the Category field, select Administrative. F09-004 (paper) may only be used in the event that the Maximo application is unavailable. If using F09-004 (paper): Obtain a unique MOC number. COMPILE the MOC package. ATTACH relevant documents to the MOC, including: Redlined drawings Redlined procedures Other items, as needed For CEP changes, ATTACH to the MOC: Amended CEP Any documentation that confirms delivery of corresponding information to project stakeholders affected by the change DETERMINE required Pre-Modification Reviews and assign responsible parties for each. Minimum reviews for every AMOC will be indicated or pre-populated on the F09-004 (paper) or F09-004A (Maximo app). Add additional reviews based on the scope of the change, including reviews beyond the available Pre-Modification review list. Evaluate key stakeholders and Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) to be included in the change review process. Refer to Management of Change (MOC) and Pre-Startup Safety Review (PSSR) Task Guidance for help choosing required pre-modification items/tasks. A Hazard Screening Review (HSR) is NOT normally required in the Administrative MOC process, as this level of review is covered by the Operations and Personnel Safety Reviews.
